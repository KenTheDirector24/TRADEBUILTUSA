What Is a Tape Measure?

A tape measure is a hand tool used to measure the length, width, height, or distance of an object or space. It has a flexible metal blade marked with measurement lines that retracts into a protective case. Because accurate measurements are required for nearly every project, it is one of the first tools every skilled trades student learns to use. In HVAC/R, a tape measure helps ensure equipment and materials are measured correctly before installation.
